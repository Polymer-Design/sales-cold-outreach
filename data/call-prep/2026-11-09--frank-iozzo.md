## Questions to ask

Questions marked **(from research)** are specific to Frank. The rest are the standard set.

### Vision and goals

- What's prompting a new site now? What isn't working about the current one?
- Who is the site mainly for: prospective clients, existing clients, or both? What should a prospective client do on it, like book an intro meeting or download something?
- **(from research)** Do most new clients find you through referrals, or does the website bring people in? This tells you how hard the site needs to work for him.
- Any advisor or other sites you like the look of?

### Pages (sets the tier, static pages only)

- **(from research)** Your current site has around 20 main pages. Which ones are essential at launch, and could any be merged or dropped? The answer decides Core (5 pages) vs. Custom.
- Is there one page that matters most, like the services overview or an "is this a fit" page?

### Content and CMS (doesn't change page count, does change setup work)

- **(from research)** You have about 48 blog posts and a few insights pieces. Are those all coming over, being pruned, or starting fresh? Who writes new ones, and how often?
- Who updates the site day to day, and how comfortable are they with editing tools?

### Compliance (likely hidden scope for a registered investment adviser)

- **(from research)** Does anything on the site need a compliance review before it goes live, like disclosures, performance figures or testimonials? Who does that review?
- **(from research)** Are there required disclosures or links, like Form ADV, a privacy policy or a state registration notice, that need to sit on every page?

### Integrations

- What does the site need to connect to: a client portal login, scheduling for intro meetings, a newsletter or email tool, a CRM?
- Anything you're adding or switching to in the next year?

### Platform fit (Webflow vs. Astro + Vercel + Sanity)

- Who works on the site today: just you, or a marketing person or team?
- How comfortable is whoever will update it with technical tools? A small, non-technical team points to Webflow.
- **(from research)** Your site takes about 17 seconds to load on mobile. How much does speed matter to you? A real need for speed points to Astro + Sanity.

### Timeline and decision

- Is there a date driving this, like a rebrand, a new service or the new year?
- Who else is involved in the decision, and who signs off on the design?
- Have you set a budget range? Only ask if it comes up naturally.

### After launch

- Who keeps the site updated once it's live? If he'd want ongoing help, that sets up a monthly plan later. Don't lead with it.
