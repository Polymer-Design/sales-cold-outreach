# Fispoke — migrating off Wix to a Webflow build for an embedded banking platform

## The situation
Fispoke sells an embedded banking platform to registered investment advisors (RIAs). Their
site ran on Wix: a handful of product pages, a blog, and team/partner profiles, all stuck
inside Wix's builder with no CMS and no way for Fispoke's own team to publish.

## What we built
Full rebuild off Wix onto Webflow: up to 25 pages, including a CMS-driven product suite
(six product lines), a Partners CMS, and CMS templates for Team, Insights, and Careers so
Fispoke's team can publish without touching code. Built in AEO/SEO/technical SEO and
structured data from scratch (the old site had none), plus Analytics and Search Console
integrations, custom iconography and motion graphics, a discovery/positioning workshop, and
CMS training for the team. Tier: Custom, $14,600. Projected timeline: 16-18 weeks.

## Results
Proof is thin here — the client-side metrics (post-launch traffic/conversion, organic
search movement, AI/AEO visibility, pipeline attribution) are all still marked "to confirm"
pending a GA4 pull and HubSpot reconciliation, so none of them are citable yet. A PageSpeed
comparison isn't a valid claim either — the old Wix site already scored 92 Performance.
Lean on scope and the before-state instead: no CMS, no structured data, zero organic-driven
demo requests, now a 25-page CMS-driven Webflow build with SEO/AEO built in.
