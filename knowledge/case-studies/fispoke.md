# Fispoke — migrating off Wix to a Webflow build for an embedded banking platform

## The situation
Fispoke sells an embedded banking platform to registered investment advisors (RIAs). Their
site ran on Wix: a handful of product pages, a blog, and team/partner profiles, all stuck
inside Wix's builder with no CMS and no way for Fispoke's own team to publish.

## What we built
Full rebuild off Wix onto Webflow: up to 25 pages, including a CMS-driven product suite
(six product lines), a Partners CMS, and CMS templates for Team, Insights, and Careers so
Fispoke's team can publish without touching code. Built in AEO/SEO/technical SEO and
structured data from scratch (the old site had none), plus Analytics and Search Console
integrations, custom iconography and motion graphics, a discovery/positioning workshop, and
CMS training for the team. Tier: Custom, $14,600. Projected timeline: 16-18 weeks.

## Results
Launched July 23, 2026. First-100-days numbers, verified:
- 7x more site traffic by average sessions per day: 2,155 sessions in the 90 days before
  launch (Wix) vs. 17,000 sessions in the first 100 days post-launch (GA4). That post-launch
  total includes Fispoke's own launch email campaign and LinkedIn posts, so say so wherever
  the full 7x number is used. Engaged sessions alone (4,100, 23.4% engagement rate) still beat
  the old all-sessions daily pace by 73%.
- 25% more demo requests per month: 32 Request a Demo submissions in the 12 months before
  launch (2.7/month) vs. 11 in the first 100 days (3.3/month). Source: HubSpot.
- Early sample, not yet citable at volume: demo-to-signed-deal time down to 9 days from an
  86-day pre-launch average. Based on only the first one or two post-launch deals — don't lead
  with this until it holds across 5+ deals.

Not citable yet: inbound-lead growth across both forms, organic-search attribution, and
AI/AEO visibility are all still "to confirm" pending more data from Brendan at Fispoke. Win
rate and page speed are explicitly not valid comparisons (sales cycle is ~86 days so most
post-launch leads are still open; the old Wix site already scored 92 Performance, so speed
isn't a before/after story here).
